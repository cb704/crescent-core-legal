3. QuickBooks Online integration

Crescent Core connects to Crescent's own QuickBooks Online company using Intuit's published

APIs under Crescent's Intuit developer credentials.

  • The integration is read-only. It requests the QuickBooks Online Accounting scope and

performs no create, update or delete operations against QuickBooks data.

  • It reads only Crescent's own accounting records. It does not access, and has no means of

accessing, any other Intuit customer's data.

  • Data retrieved is used to answer internal reporting questions — profit and loss, job costs,

receivables and payables. It is not resold, licensed, published, or shared outside Crescent.

  • Authorization is by OAuth 2.0. Access and refresh tokens are stored on Crescent-controlled

systems in the operating system keychain, are never transmitted to any third party, and are

revoked when Crescent disconnects the application.
