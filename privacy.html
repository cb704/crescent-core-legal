Privacy Policy — Crescent Core

Crescent Homes, Inc. · Last updated 8 September 2026

Who this covers

Crescent Core is private, internal software used only by Crescent Homes, Inc. It has no public

users, no sign-ups, and no consumer-facing features. It collects no information from members of

the public.

What data it accesses

Crescent Core reads Crescent's own business records from systems Crescent already owns or

subscribes to, including its QuickBooks Online company. From QuickBooks it reads accounting

data — accounts, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments and financial reports — for

internal reporting.

The QuickBooks integration is read-only. It performs no writes to QuickBooks data.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, license or share data with any third party.
  • It does not use QuickBooks data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or model training.
  • It does not access any Intuit customer's data other than Crescent's own company.
  • It does not collect information from the public, and has no public interface.

Where data lives

Data read from QuickBooks is used to answer a question and is not accumulated into a separate

product. Any cached results and all OAuth credentials are stored on Crescent-controlled

computers, with credentials held in the operating system keychain rather than in files.

Retention and deletion

Cached accounting data is retained only as long as it is operationally useful and is deleted

when no longer needed. Disconnecting Crescent Core from QuickBooks Online revokes its tokens

and ends its access immediately.

Security

Access is limited to authorized Crescent personnel. Credentials are stored in the operating

system keychain, transmitted only to Intuit over TLS 1.2 or higher, and never written to

source control or shared with third parties.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
